# 📊 Employee Workforce Analysis | Excel Dashboard

An interactive **Employee Workforce Analysis Dashboard** built entirely using **Microsoft Excel** to analyze workforce data, employee distribution, salaries, performance, overtime, leaves, and other HR-related metrics.

The project demonstrates the use of **Power Query, Power Pivot, DAX Measures, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and interactive Excel dashboards** to transform raw employee data into meaningful business insights.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Employee Workforce Analysis Dashboard** provides a comprehensive view of workforce performance and employee-related metrics.

The dashboard is divided into **3 analytical pages**, each focusing on a different aspect of the workforce:

### 1️⃣ Overview
Provides a high-level overview of the workforce, including:

- Total Employees
- Total Annual Salary
- Average Monthly Salary
- Average Job Rate
- Average Experience
- Employee distribution by Department
- Employee distribution by Experience Group
- Employee distribution by Country
- Employee distribution by Gender
- Total Employees by Year

### 2️⃣ Department Analysis
Focuses on employee performance and workforce metrics across departments:

- Total Employees
- Average Monthly Salary
- Average Job Rate
- Total Overtime
- Total Leaves
- Average Job Rate by Department
- Top 10 Departments by Average Annual Salary
- Average Overtime Hours by Department
- Total Leaves by Department

### 3️⃣ Salary & Performance Analysis
The Salary and Performance page provides deeper analysis of employee compensation, job rates, experience, and overtime.

It includes:

- **Percentage Employee by Salary Classification**
- **Total Employee by Overtime Category**
- **Percentage Employee by Job Rate Category**
- **Average Monthly Salary & Average Job Rate by Experience Groups**
- **Overtime Hours by Job Rate Category**

This page helps analyze how employee salary classification, job rate, experience, and overtime are distributed across the workforce.

## 🧮 DAX & Power Pivot

Power Pivot was used to create **7 DAX Measures** for the dashboard.

These measures were used to calculate and analyze important workforce metrics such as:

- Employee Count
- Total Annual Salary
- Average Monthly Salary
- Average Job Rate
- Average Experience
- Total Overtime
- Total Leaves

The measures were then connected to Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts to create the interactive dashboard.
